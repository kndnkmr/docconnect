# ProMedicoz — Instagram Reel Scripts (Batch 1)

These 3 scripts are built from the blog's **top-read** articles (live read data), so they
target topics the audience already cares about. Each Reel teases the single most useful
point and drives to the full article via the bio link.

**Format conventions**
- Length: 20–35 seconds (Reels under ~35s get watched fully → better reach).
- The **first 2 seconds (the hook) decide everything** — lead with it, no logo intro.
- Language: **Hinglish** (Roman) — matches the audience and the site's bilingual content.
- Shoot vertical 9:16. On-screen text should be large, high-contrast, 3–6 words per beat.
- Always end on the CTA + "link in bio." Keep the ProMedicoz logo to the last 2–3s only.
- Health-safety: these are awareness, not diagnosis. Keep the "see a doctor" line in.

**Universal caption footer (paste at end of every caption):**
> ⚕️ General awareness, not medical advice. Full guide on promedicoz.in (link in bio).
> Consult a doctor for your situation.

**Core hashtag set (mix 8–12 per post; swap the topic-specific ones):**
`#ProMedicoz #HealthTips #HealthAwareness #IndiaHealth #DoctorOnline #Swasthya #SehatTips`

## How to produce these fast (batching plan)
1. **Film 3–5 in one sitting** — same setup, same outfit changes optional. Reels don't need
   to be polished; authentic-to-camera or simple text-over-broll both work.
2. **Two easy formats if you don't want to be on camera:**
   - **Text-over-stock:** free vertical clips (Pexels/Pixabay) + big on-screen text + trending audio.
   - **Talking head:** phone on a stand, read the voiceover, add captions in the Instagram/CapCut editor.
3. **Post cadence:** 2–3 Reels/week is plenty to start. Consistency > volume.
4. **Always** put the current article link in your bio (or a Linktree) and say "link in bio."
5. **Reuse:** the same script works as a YouTube Short and a WhatsApp status.
