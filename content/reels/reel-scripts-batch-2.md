# ProMedicoz — Instagram Reel Scripts (Batch 2)

Six more ready-to-film scripts, drawn from high-appeal, highly-shareable articles. This batch
deliberately mixes Reel types so the feed stays varied: a myth-buster, a checklist/curiosity Reel,
two life-saving/emergency Reels, a seasonal awareness Reel, and a relatable everyday one.

**Same conventions as Batch 1** (see reel-scripts-batch-1.md): 20–35s, vertical 9:16, hook in the
first 2 seconds, Hinglish voiceover, big on-screen text, end on CTA + "link in bio," logo only in
the last 2–3s. These are awareness, not diagnosis — keep the "see a doctor" line.

**Universal caption footer (paste at end of every caption):**
> ⚕️ General awareness, not medical advice. Full guide on promedicoz.in (link in bio).
> Consult a doctor for your situation.

**Core hashtag set:** `#ProMedicoz #HealthTips #HealthAwareness #IndiaHealth #DoctorOnline #Swasthya #SehatTips`

## Posting plan reminder
- 2–3 Reels/week; mix the types (symptom, life-saving, curiosity, awareness, conversion).
- Keep the current article link in bio (or a Linktree) and always say "link in bio."
- Life-saving Reels (stroke, choking, heart attack) get the most shares — post those when you want reach.
- Re-check top-read articles every few weeks and build the next batch from whatever is climbing.
